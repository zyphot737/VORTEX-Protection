Protection & security

Built to survive the worst day.

The moment something looks like an attack, VORTEX Protection acts first and explains itself after.

⚡

Anti-nuke

Mass bans, kicks, channel or role deletions and webhook spam are caught in seconds — the attacker is stopped and their webhooks wiped automatically.

🔒

Lockdown mode

When things escalate, the server locks itself down, holding messages and invites until a moderator confirms it's safe.

↺

Channel & role recovery

Deleted channels and roles are snapshotted the moment they vanish. One command rebuilds them with permissions intact.

🚩

Threat registry

A shared list of known bad actors. When someone flagged joins your server, your team is alerted instantly — and can auto-act.

🧹

Word & link filter

Block banned words, invite links, scam domains and mass mentions on new messages and edits, with role exemptions for staff.

📶

Anti-spam

Message flooding earns an automatic timeout, on thresholds you tune per server.

Moderation toolkit

Everything your mods reach for.

Fast commands, a full case history, and a place for members to appeal — so the team spends less time arguing about what happened.

🛠️

Mod commands

Timeout, kick, ban (even by ID), unban and purge — with preset reasons, DM notices to the member, and a full embed log of every action.

⚠️
PREMIUM

Warnings

Warn members and let repeat warnings escalate to a timeout, kick or ban on a ladder you define.

📋

Case history

Every action becomes a numbered case. Pull a member's full history or look up any single case in one command.

🎫

Tickets

A dropdown panel lets members open a private support channel by category. On premium your team can read and reply from this site too, and closed tickets keep a transcript.

📝

Ban appeals

Banned members appeal on this site through a link made for your server. Finished appeals land in your appeal channel for the team to review.

🧯
PREMIUM

Quarantine

Strip a suspicious member's roles and lock them to one channel while you investigate — then restore every role in a click.
